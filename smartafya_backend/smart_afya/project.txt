# 🚨 CRITICAL CLIENT REQUIREMENTS (DO NOT IGNORE)

## 1. PATIENT CONSENT (VERY IMPORTANT)

* Before submitting mental health challenges:

  * Patient MUST accept Terms & Conditions
  * Store:

    * consent_given (boolean)
    * consent_timestamp
* Block submission if not accepted

---

## 2. PATIENT CHOICE OF SPECIALIST

* Patient can:

  * Select a specific doctor (if known)
  * Or leave unassigned for admin
* Include:

  * influencer specialists / clerics support

---

## 3. ADMIN RESTRICTIONS

* Admin:

  * CAN view doctor reports
  * CANNOT edit or delete doctor reports

---

## 4. ENHANCED FEEDBACK SYSTEM

Feedback must include:

* Treatment feedback (doctor performance)
* App feedback (UX, features)
* Allow extensible structure (future improvements)

---

## 5. SPECIALIST TRANSFER FEATURE

Doctor can:

* Transfer patient to another specialist

System must:

* Update session automatically
* Notify:

  * Admin
  * Patient
* Log transfer history

---

## 6. DOCTOR UNAVAILABILITY SYSTEM

Doctor can:

* Mark themselves unavailable for a session

System must:

* Notify patient
* Provide options:

  1. Transfer to another specialist
  2. Reschedule session

---

## 7. PHYSICAL SESSION PAYMENT LOGIC

* Payment is NOT fixed
* Payment is agreed manually between:

  * Admin
  * Patient
* Doctor must:

  * Be notified
  * Accept session
